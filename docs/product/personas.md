# Personas

## Maya — the plateaued intermediate (primary)

- Climbs V3–V5 indoors, 3× a week, often alone after work.
- 162 cm, ape index +2. Regularly finds that "the beta everyone uses" doesn't work for her reach.
- Wants: alternatives for shorter climbers; to *see* body position, not read about it.
- Beta wins when: the video shows a high-foot or drop-knee she wouldn't have thought of.

## Diego — the ambitious beginner

- Climbs VB–V2, 6 months in. Doesn't know the vocabulary (gaston? flag?).
- Wants: plain-language cues and a clear sequence; reassurance about what's hard.
- Beta wins when: captions explain techniques in five words, and the crux is obvious.

## Ana — the route setter (secondary)

- Sets weekly at a commercial gym. Curious how a "generic" climber would read her problems.
- Wants: a quick sanity check of intended beta and grade.
- Beta wins when: the grade estimate and crux match her intent — or reveal an unintended sequence.
